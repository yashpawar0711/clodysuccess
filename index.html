Phase 01

Discover & Align

We start with a thorough strategy session to understand your business model, customer psychology, core competitors, and clear milestones. No assumptions.

  • Goals & scope documentation
  • Target audience persona mapping

Phase 02

Plan & Design

We design intuitive wireframes and high-fidelity visual concepts. You review interactive prototypes to ensure the aesthetic and user flow match your vision.

Phase 03

Develop & Test

We write clean, modular, production-grade code. We optimize assets for sub-second loading, ensure cross-browser compatibility, and verify accessibility.

Launch & Support

We handle domain routing, SSL certificate setup, SEO indexing, and production deployment. After launch, we provide continuous technical assistance.

  • Live domain & SSL deployment

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about our web development process, pricing, timeline, and hosting support.

Most standard projects take between 1 to 3 weeks depending on scope. A focused Starter Website or high-converting Landing Page is typically completed in 5 to 7 business days, while a comprehensive Business Website or E-Commerce platform requires 2 to 4 weeks. We provide a guaranteed delivery roadmap before kick-off.

To protect both parties, we follow an agile milestone structure: typically 50% advance to commence design and technical discovery, and the remaining 50% upon final sign-off and live deployment to your domain. For larger custom projects, 3-stage milestone schedules are available.

Absolutely. Over 70% of web traffic today originates on mobile phones. Every project we engineer at Cloudy Success is designed mobile-first, ensuring smooth thumb gestures, ultra-fast loading, clear readability, and zero horizontal scroll on all modern devices.

All our packages include up to 2-3 thorough review rounds at both the design prototype stage and post-development stage. We work closely with you via WhatsApp or video calls to incorporate your feedback until you are completely satisfied with the result.

If you already have a domain and hosting, we can deploy directly to your existing setup. If you don't, we guide you step-by-step to acquire your domain (e.g., via Cloudflare, Namecheap, or GoDaddy) and set up high-performance, cost-effective hosting (Vercel, Netlify, Hostinger, or AWS). You maintain 100% legal ownership of your accounts.

Every website launch includes 14 to 60 days of complimentary technical warranty for any bugs, minor content tweaks, and operational assistance. After that warranty period, we offer affordable monthly maintenance packages or on-demand support whenever you need updates.
